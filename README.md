**DocSpot 2.0** is a modern, unified digital healthcare platform connecting Patients, Doctors, Hospitals, Laboratories, Pharmacies, and Emergency Transit Fleets into an integrated care network.

---

## ✨ Core Platform Highlights

### 1. 🔍 Practitioner & Hospital Directory
- **Specialty & Location Search**: Discover verified doctors and hospitals by specialty, ratings, and city locations.
- **Live Emergency Bed Tracker**: Real-time ICU, Oxygen, and Ventilator bed availability monitoring across nearby clinics.

### 2. 🩺 24/7 Instant GP Teleconsultation
- **Under 60-Second Pairing**: Connect with an online General Practitioner on demand for acute health concerns.
- **Encrypted HD Video & Chat**: Secure virtual consultations directly from your browser.

### 3. 🗓️ OPD Booking & Pre-Visit Intake
- **Segmented Time Slot Matrix**: Select convenient Morning, Afternoon, or Evening consultation slots.
- **Pre-Visit Digital Intake**: Pre-fill allergies, current medications, and insurance/Ayushman Bharat ID to save waiting time.
- **Smart Cancellation Waitlist**: Receive instant alerts when a full doctor's schedule opens up.

### 4. 📂 Digital Prescription Vault & Verification
- **Verified E-Prescriptions**: View and download official prescription slips issued by your practitioners.
- **QR Code Authenticity Check**: Embedded QR verification code for pharmacy partners.
- **Direct Medicine Orders**: Order prescribed medicines directly from registered pharmacy fulfillment centers.

### 5. 🚘 Medical Transportation & Fleet Dispatch
- **🚨 911 Emergency Dispatch**: Immediate critical ambulance dispatch with vehicle selection (Basic, ICU, Ventilator) and driver status tracking.
- **🚘 Scheduled NEMT Transport**: Wheelchair van or senior cab pickup scheduled 1 hour before appointment time for post-op and dialysis visits.

---

## 🔒 Security & Privacy

DocSpot 2.0 enforces strict Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC) and industry-standard encryption to protect patient health records and medical history.

---

## 📖 Live User Guide Summary

- **For Patients**: Search doctors -> Select slot -> Optional digital intake -> Complete consultation -> Download digital prescription & order medicines.
- **For Healthcare Providers**: Manage live OPD queue -> Review pre-filled intake -> Issue digital prescriptions -> Toggle 24/7 teleconsult duty status.
- **For Hospitals**: Monitor real-time ICU & Oxygen bed occupancy with safe toggle status updates.
